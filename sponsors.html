Who came to the first Mixer

The 2026 crowd.

Wine tasting with live music and a mechanical bull draws a particular crowd: Cal Poly grads who stayed in town, young professionals from SLO and the coast, and the friends they bring. Year 1 drew about 750 of them to the Madonna Inn Expo Center on a Saturday in June.

They came in cowboy hats, stayed for the band, and photographed most of it. If you want to be in front of people who are just starting to decide what they drink, where they go out and which brands they trust, this is the room.

Audience detail from Year 1 ticketing and check-in, and the Year 2 targets, are in the sponsor deck rather than on this page.
