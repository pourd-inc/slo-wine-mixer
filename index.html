What happens at the Mixer

Year 2 is bigger, on purpose.

Wine and the band stay inside. Everything else moves outdoors. Winery lineup, activations and inclusions are announced as they are confirmed.

  1. 01

    Unlimited tastings from Central Coast wineries

    One cup opens every pouring table in the building. Paso, Edna Valley, SLO Coast and beyond, building toward 60 producers this year.

  2. 02

    Live music the whole time

    A band on stage from doors to last pour, with a dance floor in front of it.

  3. 03

    An outdoor village

    Food trucks, vendors, a photo booth and activations, with more to be announced.

Coastal Cowboy is the dress code. Hats, boots, denim, whatever the coast means to you. Same brand, same cups as Year 1.
