What happens at the Mixer

At the first Mixer: wine, music, mechanical bull.

How Year 1 ran in June 2026. The 2027 lineup of wineries and activities is confirmed closer to the date.

  1. 01

    Unlimited tastings from Central Coast wineries

    One wristband opened every pouring table in the building. Paso, Edna Valley, SLO Coast and beyond. Guests brought the glass back as many times as they liked.

  2. 02

    A live band and a DJ

    Music from doors to last call: a full band on stage and a DJ between sets.

  3. 03

    The mechanical bull

    Guests took turns on the bull between tastings, hats on, crowd cheering.

Also on the floor at Year 1: short "No Snob" wine classes that teach you what you actually like, photo backdrops and neon, and the Wine Window, a walk-up pour done Central Coast style.
