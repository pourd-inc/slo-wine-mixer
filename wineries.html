Participation basics

What it takes to pour.

Booths are capped at 65 wineries. The fee, load-in hours and full terms are on the signup page before you pay.

Your booth

A 10x10 space with an 8-foot table, ice, and two staff passes. $149, paid when you sign up.

Your wines

Pour what you sell. Tell us the wines and roughly how many bottles when you confirm; we plan glassware and ice around it.

Your people

Pourers must hold a California RBS certification. Two people per table is the comfortable number for a full afternoon.

Paperwork

A certificate of insurance, uploaded once on your winery page in pourd. No forms to re-enter.

Selling on site

Bottle sales, orders and club sign-ups at your table: what is permitted is spelled out in the terms, together with the venue's rules.

The date

Saturday, April 3, 2027, 2–5 PM at the Madonna Inn Expo Center. Wine and the band are inside; food trucks and activations move to an outdoor village.

What you get back

Take home a report on your wines.

SLO Wine Mixer runs on pourd, software for wineries. At check-in your bottles are counted onto an iPad; at check-out they are counted again. Guests can rate what they taste and say whether they would buy or visit. After the event you get your own report.

What is measured: bottles checked in and out, and the ratings and intent guests choose to submit. It does not track individual guests table to table, and it does not sell you anyone's contact details.
