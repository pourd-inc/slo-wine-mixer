Participation basics

What it takes to pour.

The specifics, including the table fee, load-in hours and terms, are in the winery details packet. Request it and we send it the same week.

Your table

One pouring table with signage space. Table size, what we supply and what you bring are listed in the packet.

Your wines

Pour what you sell. Tell us the wines and roughly how many bottles when you confirm; we plan glassware and ice around it.

Your people

Pourers must hold a California RBS certification. Two people per table is the comfortable number for a full afternoon.

Paperwork

A certificate of insurance, uploaded once on your winery page whenever it is ready. No forms to re-enter.

Selling on site

Bottle sales, orders and club sign-ups at your table: what is permitted is spelled out in the packet, together with the venue's rules.

The date

The 2027 date is announced October 12. Requesting details now is not a commitment; you confirm once you have the date and the terms.

What you get back

Take home a report on your wines.

The Mixer runs on pourd, software for wineries. At check-in your bottles are counted onto an iPad; at check-out they are counted again. Guests can rate what they taste and say whether they would buy or visit. After the event you get your own report.

What is measured: bottles checked in and out, and the ratings and intent guests choose to submit. It does not track individual guests table to table, and it does not sell you anyone's contact details.
